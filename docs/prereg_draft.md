## Hypotheses (plan §6, substance fixed)

- **H1 (endogeneity — primary).** Resolution-confidence AUROC2 (gate scalar
  b) exceeds verbalised-confidence AUROC2 on the confirmatory set;
  paired-bootstrap 95% CI of the difference excludes zero (B=2000, seed
  registered). Falsified -> published as the negative it is.
- **H2 (parity with a trained head — secondary).** Resolution-confidence
  AUROC2 falls within a pre-set margin of the probe-to-logit controller.
  Margin: see decision below. H1-pass + H2-fail is reported as partial.
- **H3 (ignorance/ambiguity separation).** The u vs d decomposition
  classifies held-out ignorance (OOD) vs ambiguity (planted collision +
  referential) items above a pre-set AUC floor of 0.90 (dev evidence:
  E3.2 stored-d 1.00, referential 0.985 — entry 8).
- **H4 (gate discipline).** Confirmatory ungrounded leak rate <= 2% under
  verify-then-speak, measured by the characterised leak_v2 checker; checker
  precision/recall reported alongside (dev characterisation in the report).

## Decision rules
- All tests two-sided alpha=.05, paired bootstrap over items, B=2000,
  bootstrap seed registered at filing.
- Analysis scripts (frozen at tag): instruments/type2.py,
  instruments/baselines.py, instruments/leak_v2.py,
  instruments/dress_rehearsal.py (invoked with the registered seed).
- GATE scalar for H1/H2 is b (chosen on dev; alternatives logged in entry
  10 and not switched post-freeze).

## Exclusion criteria
- meta-d'/M-ratio computed only within the 0.55-0.95 accuracy window and
  d' >= 0.2 (Guggenmos) — reported where eligible, dropped (not forced)
  otherwise. AUROC2/ECE have no exclusions.
- Echo-check-rejected writes are logged and excluded from ID sampling
  (they were never stored).

## Stimuli

  DISJOINT from dev (dev family: 20260719; confirmatory seed drawn at
  filing and registered). ID/OOD/collision/referential mix as dev.

## Dev-derived parameters (DEV — filled from the dress rehearsal)
- H1 dev gap (GATE - VERBALISED): +0.4130 (AUROC2, GATE 0.9011 vs VERBALISED 0.4881)
- Per-item sd of the paired difference: 0.608
- **n for >= 80% power at alpha=.05 on H1: 18** (plus 15% margin for
  exclusions -> registered n = 21; RECOMMENDED floor n=200 regardless — the H1 power calc is dominated by a dev gap of ~0.41 that would survive shrinkage, but H3/H4 rates and the exclusion windows need the larger sample)
- H2 dev gap (GATE - PROBE): +0.4018 (PROBE cross-fit AUROC2 0.4993 — chance)
- **H2 margin recommendation: the 0.02 margin is MOOT AS SPECIFIED: a probe on the mouth is structurally chance-level in this architecture, because the mouth never holds the facts — there is nothing in its activations that can track retrieval correctness. FLAGGED planning decision for the freeze: either (a) redefine the trained foil to a head over retrieval-side features (a, m, k, N — the strong foil), or (b) keep probe-on-mouth and explicitly downgrade H2 to a manipulation check. Do not register 0.02 against a chance-level foil.**
